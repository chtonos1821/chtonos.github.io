01
Spanish Tax Compliance

AEAT • TGSS • IRPF • IVA

Spanish Tax Compliance Built for International Professionals

Navigating the Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT) requires more than translating forms. I manage your ongoing compliance, digital certificates, and residency position with rigorous empirical accuracy.

Autónomo & SL Setup & Lifecycle Management

01.1

Complete registration and deregistration (alta/baja) with AEAT and Social Security (TGSS), census declarations (Modelo 036), activity classification (IAE), and EORI registration for cross-border EU trade.

Quarterly & Annual Tax Filings

01.2

Accurate preparation and electronic submission of VAT (Modelos 303, 390, 349), personal income tax installments (Modelo 130), withholdings (Modelos 111, 115, 180, 190), annual informative returns (Modelo 347), and annual IRPF (Modelo 100) or Non-Resident Tax (Modelo 210).

Tax Residency & Cross-Border Diagnostics

01.3

Technical evaluation of Spanish tax residency exposure under Law 35/2006 (183-day rule, center of economic interests, and family ties) before you trigger unintended worldwide taxation.

Digital Representation & AEAT Audit Defense

01.4

Configuration and management of your FNMT digital certificate, Cl@ve, and mandatory electronic notification inbox (DEHú) so no official tax notice goes unanswered.
